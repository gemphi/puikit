# HERO
**Micro-label:** LEGAL & TRANSPARENCY
**Headline:** An agency that inspects others must be glass itself.

# SECTION — TRANSPARENCY COMMITMENTS

- **Funding.** Quarterly disclosure of all funding sources and amounts; 15%
  single-source cap from year two (Charter Art. 3).
- **Decisions.** Every certification, condition, suspension and revocation
  published within 24 hours with a named responsible officer.
- **Minutes.** Board and Assembly minutes published, redactions justified
  in-line.
- **Annual report.** Finances, evaluation statistics, incident summaries,
  audit letter.

# SECTION — CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST POLICY

Five-year recusal rule for evaluators; divestment requirements for
Directorate leadership; public COI register for all council members.

# SECTION — TERMS OF USE (summary + full text)

Certification marks: licensed, revocable, class-visible. Site content: may

# SECTION — PRIVACY POLICY (summary + full text)

Minimal collection: inquiry form data used solely to respond; no ad
trackers; analytics self-hosted and aggregate; incident-report identities
protected, whistleblower channel sealed from all non-triage staff.
