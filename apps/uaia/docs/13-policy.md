# HERO
**Micro-label:** POLICY TOPICS
**Headline:** The agenda the frontier era demands.

**Intro:** These are the policy tracks UAIA works — the questions that the
global AI-safety community, from frontier labs to philanthropies to
international bodies, has pushed to the front of the agenda. On each, UAIA
publishes its own position and the strongest opposing view.

# SECTION — POLICY TRACKS (card grid, each links to a detail anchor)

**P-01 · Frontier safety cases.** Before R3/R4 deployment, developers present
a structured safety case; UAIA's role is adversarial review, not rubber
stamps.

**P-02 · Compute thresholds & notification.** Training-compute triggers for
mandatory notification; why FLOP thresholds are a tripwire, not a verdict.

**P-03 · Responsible scaling.** Tier-gated capability commitments: what must
be true, and demonstrated, before the next scale-up ships.

**P-04 · Provenance & watermarking.** Content authenticity infrastructure as
a public good; STD-310 as the interoperable floor.

**P-05 · Open-weights policy.** Open release evaluated as a deployment
surface with irreversible-diffusion analysis — neither exempt nor banned.

**P-06 · AI for global health & climate.** Fast-track evaluation lanes for
models serving vaccination logistics, disease surveillance, agricultural
resilience and climate forecasting — rigor without delay where AI can save
lives; equity of access as a certification consideration.

**P-07 · Agentic systems in the economy.** Autonomy ceilings by deployment
context; audit trails for consequential automated decisions.

**P-08 · Evaluation access & national capacity.** Shared eval infrastructure
so that safety oversight is not a privilege of the largest economies.

# SECTION — HOW POSITIONS ARE MADE

Draft position → public comment → Assembly review → published with dissents.
Every position page carries a "strongest counter-argument" section, on
principle.
