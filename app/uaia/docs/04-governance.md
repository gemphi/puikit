# HERO
**Micro-label:** GOVERNANCE & CHARTER
**Headline:** Structured so that no one — including us — has to be trusted blindly.

# SECTION — THE THREE CHAMBERS

**The Board.** Sets strategy, ratifies standards, appoints the Directorate.
Founding seats are in appointment (see /leadership). No sitting executive of
a model developer may hold a Board seat.

**The Technical Directorate.** Runs UAIA-EVAL, the Registry and certification.
Led by the Chief Technology Officer. Evaluators are firewalled from funding
and from the entities they evaluate.

**The Public Assembly.** A rotating council of civil-society, academic and
international observers that reviews decisions, publishes dissents, and can
force a re-evaluation with a two-thirds vote.

# SECTION — CHARTER (KEY ARTICLES, SUMMARIZED)

- **Art. 1 — Mandate.** Evaluate, certify, authorize; nothing else.
- **Art. 3 — Independence.** Funding is disclosed quarterly; no single source
  may exceed 15% of the annual budget after year two.
- **Art. 5 — Conflicts.** Evaluators recuse from any model they contributed to
  within five years.
- **Art. 7 — Publicity.** Certificates, revocations and standards are public
  by default; only narrow security-sensitive eval details may be sealed, with
  the Assembly's countersignature.
- **Art. 9 — Revision.** The charter itself is versioned and amendable only
  with Board supermajority plus Assembly assent.

# SECTION — DECISION FLOW (diagram spec)

Developer submission → Directorate evaluation → Certification decision →
Assembly review window (14 days) → Registry publication → Continuous
monitoring → (incident) → Review → Amend / Suspend / Revoke.

# SECTION — ACCOUNTABILITY

Annual external audit; public minutes; every authorization decision carries a
named responsible officer and a machine-readable evidence bundle.
