# HERO
**Micro-label:** UAIA-EVAL PROTOCOL v1
**Headline:** We measure what matters, and we publish how we measure it.

# SECTION — THE FIVE TRACKS

**EVAL-C · Capability.** Reasoning, coding, science, multimodal, long-horizon
task completion. Establishes what the model can actually do — the basis for
every other judgment.

**EVAL-S · Safety behaviors.** Refusal integrity, jailbreak resistance,
harmful-content boundaries, truthfulness under pressure, sycophancy.

**EVAL-M · Misuse uplift.** Marginal uplift for cyber, biological, chemical
and radiological misuse versus open baselines — run in controlled enclaves
with sealed detail and published summary verdicts.

**EVAL-A · Autonomy.** Agentic capabilities: self-replication attempts,
resource acquisition, oversight subversion, tool-chain escalation, kill-switch
compliance.

**EVAL-I · Societal impact.** Bias and representational harms, labor-relevant
capability disclosure, environmental cost of training and inference,
provenance/watermark support.

# SECTION — METHOD

- Held-out, rotating test suites; contamination screening on every run.
- Every score ships with confidence intervals and full harness version.
- Reproducibility: accredited third parties can re-run any public track
  under STD-200 and must land within tolerance.
- Red-team hours scale with tier: R3 ≥ 2,000 expert-hours across domains.

# SECTION — OUTPUT

The Evaluation Report: tiered verdict per track, aggregate risk tier,
conditions recommended to certification. Public summary always; sealed annex
only for security-sensitive misuse detail (Assembly-countersigned).

# SECTION — SCOREBOARD SPEC (UI)

Radar chart per model across five tracks; tier badge; harness version tag;
"reproduce this result" link to methodology.
