# HERO
**Micro-label:** RESEARCH & PUBLICATIONS
**Headline:** Our evaluations are only as good as our science.

# SECTION — PUBLICATION TRACKS

- **Technical Reports (UAIA-TR).** Full methodology of each UAIA-EVAL track;
  harness architecture; contamination-screening methods.
- **The State of Model Safety (annual).** Aggregate registry findings: tier
  distributions, incident statistics, year-over-year safety trends.
- **Working Papers.** Open problems: agentic evaluation, capability
  forecasting, eval saturation, sealed-annex governance.

# SECTION — RESEARCH AGENDA (cards)

1. **Forecast-grade evaluation.** Moving from "what can it do today" to
   calibrated prediction of capability at the next compute scale — drawing on
   the operator-learning and simulation methods transforming forecasting in
   the physical sciences.
2. **Autonomy metrology.** Standard units for agentic capability: task
   horizon, oversight-subversion resistance, kill-switch latency.
3. **Reproducibility at frontier scale.** Tolerance bands when inference is
   stochastic and hardware heterogeneous.
4. **Societal-impact instrumentation.** Measuring labor and information-
   ecosystem effects with the rigor of capability benchmarks.

# SECTION — OPEN INFRASTRUCTURE

Harnesses and public test suites are released under open licenses after each
rotation cycle. Accredited re-runners get early access under STD-200.
