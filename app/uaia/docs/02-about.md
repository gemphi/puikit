**Headline:** One agency. One question: is this model safe enough to ship?

# SECTION — WHAT UAIA IS

The Unified Artificial Intelligence Agency is an independent, non-governmental
regulatory and standards body for artificial intelligence models. It was
declared on 11 September 2026 to provide what no single company, government or
academic group can: a unified, technically rigorous, publicly accountable
gate between AI development and AI deployment.

UAIA does three things and only three things: it **evaluates** models, it
**certifies** the ones that pass, and it **authorizes** their presence on the
market under continuous monitoring.

# SECTION — THE AUTHORIZATION GAP

Frontier models today reach billions of users on the strength of internal
review alone. Every other technology of comparable consequence — aircraft,
pharmaceuticals, nuclear reactors, financial exchanges — passes through an
independent authority first. AI's "authorization gap" is the space between
what developers self-report and what the public can verify. UAIA is built to
occupy exactly that space.

# SECTION — WHAT UAIA IS NOT

- Not a developer of models. UAIA builds evaluation harnesses, never products.
- Not a government body. It is independent, funded transparently (see /legal).
- Not a brake on science. Research is not regulated; market deployment is.
