# HERO
**Micro-label:** MISSION & VISION
**Headline:** The market for intelligence must be earned.

# SECTION — MISSION

To ensure that every artificial intelligence model offered to the public has
been independently evaluated, transparently certified, and is continuously
monitored — so that the benefits of AI compound and its failures never do.

# SECTION — VISION 2030

By 2030, "UAIA-certified" is understood the way "airworthy" is understood:
a claim no serious deployer ships without, verified by an authority no single
interest controls, recognized across borders through agreements with the
United Nations system and national regulators.

# SECTION — THE SEVEN PRINCIPLES

1. **Independence.** No developer, state or funder directs an evaluation's outcome.
2. **Evidence over assurance.** We measure; we do not take anyone's word.
3. **Proportionality.** Obligations scale with risk tier — a toy is not a frontier system.
4. **Transparency.** Every certificate, standard and revocation is public.
5. **Reproducibility.** Any qualified third party can re-run our evaluations.
6. **Global equity.** Safety must not become a moat; access programs keep
   certification reachable for labs outside the largest ecosystems.
7. **Humility.** Standards version; science moves; UAIA revises in public.

# SECTION — WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

- A public registry the world checks before it deploys.
- Incidents that fall year over year while certified deployments rise.
- A generation of models where safety work is a market advantage, not a tax.
