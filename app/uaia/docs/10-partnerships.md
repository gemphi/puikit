# SECTION — THE UNITED NATIONS ENGAGEMENT PROGRAM

UAIA's founding declaration commits the agency to seek formal engagement with
the United Nations system. Stated plainly: this is a program we are pursuing,
not a partnership already concluded — and this page will always say exactly
which it is.

The program's goals, in order:
1. Observer participation in UN AI-governance dialogues and the bodies
   emerging from them.
2. Alignment of UAIA-STD with UN-endorsed AI principles, mapped clause by
   clause and published.
3. A mutual-recognition pathway so UAIA certificates can serve member states
   that lack national evaluation capacity.
4. A standing offer: UAIA-EVAL infrastructure, at cost, for any UN program
   assessing AI systems used in humanitarian and development work.

Status board UI: each goal with `STATUS: INITIATED / IN DIALOGUE / AGREED`
(all begin at INITIATED).

# SECTION — NATIONAL REGULATORS

Mutual-recognition doctrine: UAIA seeks bilateral technical agreements —
shared harnesses under STD-200, registry mirroring under STD-500 — so a model
evaluated once is understood everywhere.

# SECTION — LABS & ACADEMIA

Developers join through the evaluation program itself; academic groups join
through harness contributions and the reproducibility network. Contributions
are credited on the registry; endorsements are never implied.

# SECTION — GLOBAL EQUITY TRACK

Fee waivers and evaluation-capacity grants for laboratories in
under-resourced ecosystems, so certification is a bridge, not a moat.
