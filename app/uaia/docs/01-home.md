# SECTION — THE THREE MANDATES

**EVALUATE.** Every frontier model is measured against the UAIA-EVAL protocol:
capability, safety, misuse resistance, autonomy and societal impact.

**CERTIFY.** Models that meet the bar receive a public, revocable certificate
tied to an entry in the open Model Registry.

**AUTHORIZE.** Market access follows certification — scoped to risk tier,
deployment context and monitoring obligations.

# SECTION — WHY UAIA EXISTS

Aviation has its authority. Medicine has its agencies. Nuclear energy has its
inspectorate. Artificial intelligence — the most general technology ever
built — has had none. UAIA exists to close that gap: one unified, technically
sovereign body whose only product is trust.
